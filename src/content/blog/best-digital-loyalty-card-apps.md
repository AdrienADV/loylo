Paper stamp cards are cheap, but they get lost, copied and forgotten. Digital loyalty cards fix that, and most of them now live where your customers already keep their tickets: Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.

There are dozens of tools out there, and most "best of" lists are written by one of them. This one is too: we make Loylo. So we kept the comparison to facts you can check on each tool's site, and we say plainly where another tool is a better fit than ours.

## What to look for in a loyalty card app

Before you compare names, decide what your counter needs:

- **No app for your customers.** Every app download you ask for loses sign-ups. A card that goes straight into Apple Wallet or Google Wallet asks for nothing.
- **No new hardware.** Your phone's camera can scan a card. A tool that needs its own tablet or stamp device adds cost and one more thing to charge.
- **Fast enrollment.** A QR code by the till, a name and an email: the whole sign-up should fit in the time it takes to make a coffee.
- **A way to talk to your regulars.** A message on the card, for a new product or today's opening hours, brings people back better than any discount.
- **A price that fits one shop.** Some tools charge per location, some per customer, some only come with a point-of-sale system.

## 1. Loylo

Loylo is a loyalty card for small shops that lives in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. You design the card with your name, color and logo, print a QR code, and customers add the card from their own phone. At the counter, you scan it with your phone's camera and add or redeem points; the card updates in their wallet right away.

- **Best for:** coffee shops, bakeries, food trucks and independent shops that want a points card running today, without hardware or a new POS.
- **What stands out:** customers only type their name and email; you send a message to every card, shown on the card with a notification, once a day at most; you invite your staff by email; every points change stays in each member's history.
- **Keep in mind:** Loylo is deliberately simple. It counts points, it doesn't connect to your till, and it doesn't run automated campaigns or tiers.

## 2. Loopy Loyalty

Loopy Loyalty is one of the longest-running digital stamp card tools. Cards go to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet without an app, and staff stamp them by scanning.

- **Best for:** shops that want a classic stamp card, digitized, with lots of design templates.
- **Keep in mind:** it's built around stamps rather than points, and its plans are paid after a free trial, with some features kept for higher tiers.

## 3. Square Loyalty

If you already take payments with Square, Square Loyalty plugs straight into your checkout: customers enter their phone number when they pay and earn points automatically.

- **Best for:** businesses already on Square POS that want loyalty with no extra step at the till.
- **Keep in mind:** it only works with Square's point of sale, it's billed per location as an add-on, and customers don't get a card in their wallet.

## 4. BoomerangMe

BoomerangMe offers many card types, such as stamps, points, discounts and memberships, all delivered to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.

- **Best for:** businesses that need several card mechanics, and agencies that manage loyalty for many clients.
- **Keep in mind:** the product leans toward agencies and resellers, so a single shop may pay for more than it uses.

## 5. PassKit

PassKit is a platform for creating and managing wallet passes of every kind: loyalty cards, coupons, tickets and memberships.

- **Best for:** businesses with a developer, or agencies building custom wallet experiences.
- **Keep in mind:** it's a toolkit more than a ready-made loyalty program. A small shop will need someone to set it up.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do customers need to download an app?

Not with Loylo, Loopy Loyalty or BoomerangMe: the card goes into Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, which are already on the phone. Square Loyalty uses the customer's phone number instead of a card.

### What do I need at the counter?

With Loylo, a phone with a camera is enough. Other tools may ask for a specific tablet, a stamp device or their own point of sale.

### Points or stamps?

Stamps are easy to explain when every visit looks alike, like a coffee. Points work better when baskets vary, like a bakery on Sunday. Either way, keep the first reward close: a card that starts with a few welcome points gets finished more often.

### Can I switch from paper cards?

Yes. Put the QR code next to the till, and when a customer hands you a paper card, issue them a digital card with points that match their stamps. We explain how in [why paper stamp cards stop working](/blog/paper-stamp-cards).
