A food truck's best customers are the ones who come back every Tuesday. The trouble is that Tuesday's spot is not always the same, and a paper stamp card can't tell anyone where you parked.

## Keep the card where they already look

A card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet sits next to boarding passes and tickets. Customers add it once, by scanning the QR code on your menu board, and it stays on their phone: no app to download, no account to create, nothing to forget at home.

## Tell your regulars where you are

With Loylo you can send a short message to every card of your program. It shows on the card and notifies the phone, so it works for the news a food truck actually has:

- “Today at the harbor market, 11:30 to 14:30.”
- “Closed for the holidays, back on the 6th.”
- “New this week: the veggie smash, double points until Friday.”

One message a day at most: enough for the day's spot, never enough to annoy anyone.

## Fast at the window

At lunch rush nobody has time for a loyalty app. Scan the customer's card with your phone's camera, add the points, and the card updates in their wallet before they get their order. Every change stays in the history, so you can check a balance when someone asks.
