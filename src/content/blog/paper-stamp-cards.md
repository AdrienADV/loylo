The paper card with ten stamps is a coffee shop classic for a reason: it's simple, and customers understand it at once. But ask any barista how many cards end up in a wash cycle, or how many customers say “I left it in my other jacket”.

## What goes wrong with paper

- Cards get lost or forgotten, so the habit breaks.
- Stamps are easy to copy, and hard to check.
- You don't know who your regulars are, or how often they come.
- You have no way to reach them when you launch a new drink.

## What a wallet card keeps

The good part of paper is that it asks nothing of the customer. A wallet card keeps that: no app, no password. Customers scan a QR code at the counter, type their name and email, and add the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. It carries your name, your color and your logo.

## What it adds

Points update on the phone right after you scan the card. You see your members and their history, and you can send a message to every card when something's new. And since the card lives on the phone, it's there every time they are.

## Switching without losing anyone

You don't need to drop paper overnight. Put the QR code next to the till, and when a customer hands you a paper card, issue them a wallet card with the welcome points that match their stamps.
